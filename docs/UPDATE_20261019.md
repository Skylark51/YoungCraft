MPDAP and CHDAP await the user's reference files. They are not represented by guessed structures.

New ligand fragments retain internal source coordinates under rigid rotation and translation. Metal donor contacts are recorded separately from covalent bonds. Aromatic C5N rings and explicit-H valences are checked before export; source file SHA-256 and original atom numbers are recorded in each CJSON. Canonical alignment exposes the ligand's open face toward the oxygen-fragment reference frame. These structures remain starting models, especially after metal replacement.

Three oxygen species × two modes are available: peroxo, superoxo, hydroperoxo; end-on (η1) and side-on (η2). The existing side-on peroxo remains source-derived. Five new fragments use idealized M–O 1.95 Å, O–O 1.45 Å (peroxo/hydroperoxo) or 1.30 Å (superoxo), distal O–H 0.98 Å where present. End-on M–O–O is 120°, O–O–H is 104°. Superoxo's 1.5 O–O bond order is schematic. These numbers and formal charges are editable input assumptions, not computed spin/oxidation-state assignments or optimized minima.

General oxygen model reference: [Cramer et al., PNAS, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0535926100](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0535926100). This supports geometric terminology and qualitative O–O ranges, not a universal geometry for every ligand/metal.

**구조 만들기 → 리간드 템플릿 저장** saves the current selected ligand plus reference metal (or all atoms if selection-only is off) into the user's application-data `lab_fragments/ligands` folder. Solvent, substrates and adducts must be excluded from the selection. The reference metal becomes a dummy; donor links and its coordinate frame persist. Attach through the fragment browser for automatic orientation. Direct complex assembly requires a compatible metal/adduct coordinate frame. User templates override bundled templates with identical category/name. Files are written atomically.

## Scan approach preparation

Click two reactive atoms in order, then **Scan 접근 배치…** beneath the viewport or in **구조 만들기**. Set the requested reactive-atom separation. Each side is a rigid body: its reactive site's outward vector (opposite its bonded-neighbor centroid) faces the other site. The second side's roll is sampled at 5° intervals, minimizing close-contact penalties against all other atoms. Unrelated components stay fixed; coordination-site directions rotate with their component.

Works for separate fragments or a bond whose removal separates the graph. Rings/alternate connectivity paths are rejected without edits. Unavoidable contacts receive a status message. The result is a geometric starting arrangement; it does not optimize remaining internal coordinates or generate an entire PES scan. Use existing geometry controls/calculation-input export and MM/xTB afterward as appropriate. Calculation documents are copied to an editable tab, and the operation supports undo.
