The eight palettes are Classic, Lagoon, Iris, Botanical, Nordic, Candy, Ocean, and Editorial. The two color swatches show the positive phase first and negative phase second. Individual colors remain editable; unmatched pairs appear as Custom.

Choose **표면 표현** independently: Solid, Dotted, Fine Dotted, Mesh, or Contour. Dotted uses evenly spaced circular stippling; Fine Dotted uses a smaller pitch. Opacity and isovalue remain independently adjustable.

Surface patterns also apply to MEP while preserving its potential color mapping and color range. MO palettes affect MO phase colors. Surface pattern and phase colors are saved with the render style.
